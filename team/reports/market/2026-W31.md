  artists, the period immediately after a release — the first 1-2 weeks — is typically
  when whatever cross-posting/algorithmic push a new upload gets on SoundCloud and
  Instagram is strongest (fresh-upload discovery windows, follower notification reach).
  If the actual upload hasn't happened yet, that discovery window hasn't started either —
  meaning every day the `[TBD]` inputs stay unfilled isn't just a stalled task, it's actual
  lost reach relative to whenever the track does go live. This is arithmetic on a known
  mechanism, not a new trend I'm claiming to have observed — I flagged the "compounding
  cost" version of this exact mechanism for the collab-credit question in W28-W30; this is
  the same shape applied to the upload timing itself, which I hadn't stated this plainly

  way):** if the credit question resolves *after* an eventual upload rather than before
  it, the artist-field/caption text becomes something that has to be edited post-publish
  rather than set correctly once — SoundCloud and YouTube both allow metadata edits after
  upload, but a title/description change after a track has already started collecting its
  first plays or been shared with the original text visible is a real (if modest, and I
  can't quantify it) different scenario from getting it right at initial publish. This is
  a general mechanical observation about how these platforms work, not something I have
  ISΛRK-specific numbers for — flagging it because it's a new consequence of the delay
