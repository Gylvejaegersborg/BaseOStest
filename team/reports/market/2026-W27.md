## 1. Does the collab-framing thesis still hold given the date slip? Anything time-sensitive about the 7-day delay?

**The thesis itself: yes, unchanged, and this week's actual content plan is the
strongest evidence for it.** My W25 read called the ISΛRK × 10k.emraan story the
content spine, not a footnote — independent producer-to-producer collabs travel
further with the production-community audience (the realistic first audience for
Switch) than a generic beat-lease framing does. W27 is the week the arc doc was

slip from July 18 to July 25 doesn't touch any of that reasoning — the thesis was
never about the calendar date, it was about who gets credited and how loud. Nothing
about a 7-day push changes whether two producers' names together outperform one.

**Whether the 7-day slip itself changes anything market-timing-wise: I don't have a
real basis to claim it does, and I'd rather say that plainly than manufacture a take.**
Concretely, here's what I can and can't say:

- **Day-of-week.** July 18, 2026 is a Friday. July 25, 2026 is a Saturday (the arc doc
  itself notes this in its W28 section). General-knowledge pattern: Friday is the
  conventional release day for playlist-pitch-dependent strategies (aligning with
  editorial playlist update cycles) — but ISΛRK isn't on a playlist-pitch strategy here,
  this is a direct-to-fans/producer-community drop via SoundCloud/YouTube. For that
  kind of release, Saturday isn't worse — if anything, weekend posting can catch more
  leisure scroll time on IG/TikTok for an independent producer's audience. I can't say
  this is better or worse with any confidence; it's a wash on the information I have,
  and treating it as a meaningful edge in either direction would be inventing precision
  I don't have.
- **Algorithm timing.** I do not have current, verifiable information about
  SoundCloud's or YouTube's algorithm behavior around day-of-week or any other
  scheduling variable as of mid-2026. Anything I said here would be guessing dressed
  up as expertise. Needs verification before anyone acts on a day-of-week assumption —
  flagging that explicitly rather than filling the space with a plausible-sounding
  claim.
- **What the slip does change, concretely and already known:** the Switch→Homerun
  promo-window gap compresses from 21 to 14 days (calendar's own math, not mine to
  recompute). That's a scheduling/operational fact already surfaced by Hemera, not a
  new market read — flagging only that it's the one real consequence of the date move
  worth watching from a content-overlap perspective, not the day-of-week question.

**Bottom line on #1: thesis holds, no correction needed. On day-of-week/algorithm
timing specifically — I don't have grounds to say the 7-day slip helps or hurts, and

electronic/drill producer at ISΛRK's scale. Both shapes exist among comparable
independent artists; I have no live data and no catalog precedent in this repo to

final-stretch push is proven better" or "steady cadence is proven better" would both be

deciding vote between (a)/(b)/(c).

**Bottom line on #2: no real grounds to declare a "final stretch push" decisively better
