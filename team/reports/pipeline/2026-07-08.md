## The calendar gap finding — investigated directly, verdict on disposition

Read `team/reports/q3-2026-release-calendar.md` in full. Confirmed: the dated log
jumps from the 2026-07-05 entry straight to a new 2026-07-08 entry. There is no
2026-07-06 or 2026-07-07 dated entry anywhere in the file. This is real, not
overstated by Hemera.

Worth being precise about what this gap actually is, though, because "no calendar
entry" is not automatically a defect. Two kinds of work happened on those days:
Theia's W28 market read and Hermes's contacts.md/10k.emraan draft work are both
already logged in full, correctly, in their own dedicated files
(`team/reports/market/2026-W28.md`, `team/reports/contacts.md`) and in `tasks.json`'s
notes fields — nothing about them is lost or unrecoverable, they're just not
duplicated into the calendar's day-by-day log. Mnemosyne's queue-header fix is
likewise fully recorded in `tasks.json` (`t-queue-json-updatedat-staleness-0707`).
The calendar file's stated job, per its own header, is tracking dated
decisions/checkpoints on the four Q3 releases — not a duplicate meeting log of
everything every agent did. Judged against that job, a day with no release-relevant
decision genuinely may not need its own calendar entry.

The real problem is narrower than "two days of work went unlogged": it's that four
separate overlay standup notes (`os-note-theia-market-w28`, `os-note-argus-pipeline-
0706`, `os-note-hemera-standup-0707`, `os-note-argus-pipeline-0707`) all say "Full
detail: `team/reports/q3-2026-release-calendar.md`" as a footer, which is a direct
pointer promising a same-day entry that isn't there. That's a genuine drift between
what agents claim as their source-of-truth pointer and what's actually written at
that pointer — a smaller, more precise defect than "the calendar has a hole," and a
different fix. The fix is not backfilling invented July 6/7 entries (Hemera is right
to refuse that — fabricating retroactive timestamps/reasoning is worse than the gap
itself) and it is not mechanically forcing a dated entry on every silent day either
(that would manufacture busywork on days with no release-relevant decision, the same
failure mode this team has correctly avoided all month on Saturdays/Sundays). The
actual fix: the "Full detail" footer convention should only be written when a
same-day dated entry genuinely exists; on days where the standup's substance lives
elsewhere (a market report, a contacts.md entry, a task note), the footer should
point to that actual file instead of defaulting to the calendar by habit. That is a
one-line convention fix, owned by whoever writes overlay notes that day — practically
Hemera, since she authors the daily standup note.

**Filed as a task** (see below) rather than left as a plain flag, because it's a
recurring convention (footer pointer defaulting to a file with no matching entry) that
will keep producing the same false promise on future quiet-Saturday/Sunday-style days
unless the rule is stated once, in writing, the way this team has fixed every other
small convention drift this month (queue-age anchoring, overlay reminder sweeps,
queue.json header bumps).
