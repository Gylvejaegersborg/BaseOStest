Hemera did write a re-spacing entry into the Release Spacing section today, and the
substance of it is fine — she correctly states that the Sep 30 runway is "not a gap to fill
reflexively" and is explicit that nothing is being scheduled into it speculatively. That's
good, careful writing. But she's using the part-3 mechanism's name for an action the
mechanism's own text doesn't describe. Part 3 was written for compression (two things
colliding); today's action is closer to recalculation following subtraction (one thing
removed, a downstream measurement updated to match). Both are reasonable things to do.
They are not the same mechanism, and the policy's text, read literally, only commits the
team to doing the first one.

**This matters because of what it sets up next.** If "re-spacing" silently expands to mean
"any time the calendar's math needs updating," it stops being a falsifiable commitment and
becomes a label applied after the fact to whatever editing happened anyway. I'm not saying
that's happened yet — today's edit is accurate and restrained. I'm flagging that the policy's
scope, as written, is narrower than today's use of it, and the gap should be closed in the
policy's text (add "or a scheduled item drops out, changing what adjacent spacing was
measured against" as an explicit second trigger) rather than left to expand by usage. Filing
this as a task for Hemera below.
