## Track-by-Track

### 1. Switch
**Target: July 18, 2026**
Status: export done, upload scheduled.
This is the most ready asset. Upload package is being built now (Nyx). No known
creative blocker. The gap between now and July 18 gives six weeks for the upload,
thumbnail/art review, caption copy, and a short promo window (one week minimum before
release). This is the anchor release of Q3.

Blockers: none creative. Operational: upload package must be approved, then the
SoundCloud/YouTube upload must execute and return a scheduled-publish link. If the
upload package is approved by June 20, July 18 is safe. If it slips past June 27,
push to July 25.

Pre-release tasks:
- Nyx: build upload package (in progress, handed off today)
- User: approve package (target: June 20)
- Nyx: queue SoundCloud upload for scheduling

### 2. Homerun

Status: art + master done. Video pending — that is the only blocker.
Homerun is ready except for a video asset. Three weeks after Switch gives each release

for melodic/electronic releases (audience is online, editorial playlists often update

Blockers:

Pre-release tasks:

If the chorus is not done by August 1, the track moves to Q4.

Blockers:

- Master after mix
- Art (none confirmed yet)

Pre-release tasks:
