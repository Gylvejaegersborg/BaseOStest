# Switch — cover art prompt

for: SoundCloud cover (3000 × 3000 px) + YouTube thumbnail (1280 × 720 px)
gradient reference from site data: `#A78BFA` → `#150A33` (purple to near-black)

---

## visual direction

the existing site gradient gives the anchor palette: cool violet bleeding into deep indigo-black.
lean into that — this is a drill track, 186 bpm, C minor. the visual should feel fast and slightly
dangerous. not loud or cluttered. negative space does the work.

---

## square cover art prompt (for AI generation or manual design)

a single abstract form — fractured geometry or a sharp diagonal slash — set against a deep
indigo-black void. the form catches light in violet and cold electric purple, as if it has just
pivoted or cut direction. no faces, no typography, no literal imagery. the mood is: something
just changed, and you felt it before you saw it. sparse. high contrast. slightly cinematic grain.
palette: `#A78BFA`, `#7C3AED`, `#150A33`, `#0D0820`. output: 3000 × 3000 px square.

---

## YouTube thumbnail adaptation

same composition, cropped or reframed for 16:9. add the track title "SWITCH" in bold, heavy
sans-serif type (uppercase) in white or cold violet. artist credit "ISΛRK × 10k.emraan" beneath
in a smaller weight. keep the background dark — text must be legible at thumbnail scale.
optional: small "FREE BEAT" or "FREE TYPE BEAT" badge in the corner if the account uses that
convention.
